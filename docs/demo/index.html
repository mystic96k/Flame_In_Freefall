The problem

Fire behaves differently without gravity.

Earth1 g · BUOYANCY
Hot gas rises: a candle-shaped, sooty yellow flame.
Space Station0 g · NO BUOYANCY
A dim blue sphere: ventilation alone decides if it lives.
0% O₂
PMMA kept burning far below the 21% of air.
0ppm CO
Toxic gas builds up before oxygen runs out.
0s vs 70 s
Airflow direction changes the size of a fire.
Measured on the ISS and Cygnus · NASA PSI-25 (BASS-II) and PSI-98 (SAFFIRE-I)

Machine learning

Will the flame survive or go out?

CHOSEN MODEL · LOGISTIC REGRESSION
P(burn) = σ(β₀ + β₁·O₂ + β₂·ln ueff + β₃·(ln ueff)² + β₄·P)
The squared term learns the “U-curve”: too little air smothers the flame, too much blows it off.
